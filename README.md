# ¡Hola! I'm María Manuela 👋
### Biological Engineer | Bioinformatics & Data Science Enthusiast 🧬💻

I am a **biological engineer** curious about how biotechnology, data science, and bioinformatics come together to solve real-world problems — one microbe at a time. 🦠

My path has taken me from tropical field expeditions in the Colombian lowlands, hunting yeast in social wasp nests, to genomic analysis of native coffee fermentation yeast, all the way to training in a laboratory at the University of Vienna.

## 🔬 What gets me excited

*   **Genomics & Bioinformatics:** My senior thesis characterised native yeast from Colombian coffee wet fermentation, utilizing BLAST-based identification, IQ-TREE phylogenetics, genome annotation, and building a reproducible Python-automated pipeline.
*   **Wet Lab Microbiology:** Cultivation in plates, flasks, and bioreactors • DNA extraction • PCR • Western blotting.
*   **Data Done Right:** Structuring Darwin Core databases published through SiB Colombia (GBIF), alongside rigorous data integrity, validation, and QC.
